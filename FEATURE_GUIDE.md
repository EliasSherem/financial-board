# FinSight Assistant: Feature How-To Guide

Use this guide to answer “How do I…?” questions for FinSight users. It covers both the web app (left sidebar navigation) and the mobile app (bottom tabs plus drawer).

---

## Getting Around

- **Web**: Use the sidebar to jump between Dashboard, Analytics, Projects, Transactions, Invoices, Payments, Categories, Accounts, Investments, Calendar, and Settings. Notifications and language toggle are in the top bar.
- **Mobile**: Five main tabs (Analytics, Transfers, Home, Payments, Accounts). Open the drawer for Projects, Invoices, Calendar, Guide, Investments, Categories, and Settings. The floating “+” button offers quick actions like new transaction or new account.

---

## Authentication & Profile

**Log in / Sign up**
1. Enter email and password.
2. Submit to access your data.

**Update profile**
1. Open Settings.
2. Choose “Update Profile”.
3. Edit name, email, or password and save.

---

## Dashboard (Home)

Shows KPIs (balances, expenses, growth), recent activity, scheduled items, and quick shortcuts.

**Check dashboard stats**
1. Navigate to Home.
2. Review cards and charts.
3. Use quick actions to add transactions, schedule payments, or create accounts.

---

## Accounts

**Add an account**
1. Open Accounts page/tab.
2. Click or tap “Add Account”.
3. Enter account name, type, starting balance, optional bank details.
4. Save.

**View account details**
1. Select an account.
2. See balance history, linked transactions, and metadata.

---

## Transactions

**Add a transaction**
1. Choose “New Transaction”.
2. Select account, date, amount, type (Income or Expense), category, and optional project.
3. Save to post it instantly.

**Filter or search**
1. Use filters (date range, category, amount, project) to narrow results.
2. Export or print summaries as needed.

---

## Salary Automation

**Create an automated salary**
1. Go to Settings → “Automate salary income”.
2. Add a rule: name, amount, frequency (weekly, monthly, or semi-monthly), optional weekday/day, and destination account.
3. Save. The system creates a “Salary” category if you don’t already have one.

**How it works**
- On each due date, one income transaction is added automatically.
- Balances and analytics update immediately.
- The next due date advances so the salary posts only once per cycle.

---

## Recurring Payments & Bills

**Schedule a payment**
1. Open Payments.
2. Click “Add Payment”.
3. Enter name, amount, account, category, start date, and frequency. Add installments if needed.
4. Save to schedule.

**Process overdue items**
1. In Settings, choose “Process overdue recurring payments”.
2. The system posts any missed expenses and updates the schedule.

---

## Analytics

Interactive charts for balances, per-account trends, and category breakdowns.

**Use analytics**
1. Open the Analytics page/tab.
2. Hover or tap chart points to see values.
3. Switch between total balance, account timelines, and category pies.

---

## Projects & Budgets

**Track a project**
1. Open Projects.
2. Create a project with name, description, budget, timeline, and status.
3. Link transactions or payments to the project.
4. Monitor budget vs. actual spending.

---

## Invoices & Customers

**Create an invoice**
1. Go to Invoices.
2. Click “New Invoice”.
3. Add customer details, line items, discounts, and tax.
4. Save and optionally send or export.

**Manage customers**
1. Access the Customers section from Invoices.
2. Add or edit customer records for reuse.

---

## Investments

**Add an investment**
1. Open Investments.
2. Enter investment name, principal, interest rate, and start date.
3. Save and view estimated growth.

**Record a contribution**
1. Open the investment detail view.
2. Add contribution amount and date.
3. Growth estimates update automatically.

---

## Calendar & Upcoming Events

**View calendar**
1. Open Calendar.
2. See a monthly view of upcoming salaries, payments, and reminders.
3. Tap or click a day for item details.

---

## Categories

**Manage categories and subcategories**
1. Open Categories management (Settings on mobile, sidebar on web).
2. Add a category, pick an icon, and name it.
3. Add subcategories, reorder, or delete as necessary.
4. The system maintains a dedicated “Salary” category for automated income.

---

## Settings & Utilities

- **Automate salary income**: manage salary rules.
- **Process overdue payments**: catch up missed bills.
- **Rebuild balance history**: recompute monthly balances from transactions.
- **Language**: switch between English and Spanish.
- **Logout**: sign out securely.

**Rebuild balance history**
1. Open Settings.
2. Select “Rebuild balance history”.
3. Confirms and regenerates analytics data.

---

## Notifications

**View notifications**
1. Web: click the bell icon.
2. Mobile: Settings → Notifications.
3. Review income, expense, invoice, and reminder alerts.

---
